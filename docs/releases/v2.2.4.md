# dawsu! V2.2.4

- **Draw tool:** replaced the pen icon and cursor with a drumstick.
- **Info bar:** collapsing the selection details now reclaims the space, leaving a small expand handle in both editors.
- **Lane audition:** moving hits to another lane plays that lane's sample. Works with dragging and keyboard moves, and follows the Audition toggle.
- **Snap to objects:** choose **Objects** in the snap menu to align hits with circles, slider nodes and spinner ends, including those off the beat grid.
- **Insert volume:** preset the volume of newly drawn hits with **Insert volume · %**. Clear the field to follow the map volume. Your choice is remembered, and existing hits keep their volumes.
- **Protect hitnormals:** enable **Edit → Protect hitnormals from deletion** to keep base hitnormal sounds when deleting, cutting, erasing or clearing a range. Off by default; your choice is remembered.

**Windows x64:** run `dawsu-win-Setup.exe` to install or update. Or extract `dawsu-win-Portable.zip` and run `dawsu!.exe`. Existing installs can use **File → Check for updates…**. Version: **2.2.4**.
