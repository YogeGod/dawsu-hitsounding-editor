Drum hit detection model (Hitsound mode)
  DrumModel/adtof-frame-rnn.onnx is the ADTOF Frame_RNN drum transcription model,
  converted to ONNX. The trained weights are unchanged; the graph adds ADTOF's input
  filterbank. Details: DrumModel/README.md.
  Copyright Mickael Zehren, Marco Alunno and Paolo Bientinesi.
  Source: https://github.com/MZehren/ADTOF (commit b3968fb)
